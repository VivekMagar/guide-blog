Housing & Renting a Room in Romania: What Workers Must Know

Overview

Finding safe, clean, and affordable housing in Romania is critical for your health and your legal immigration status. In Romania, your residence permit (Permis de Ședere) is legally tied to your registered residential address.

Whether you live in employer-provided housing or rent an apartment privately, you must have official documentation proving you legally occupy that living space.

Employer-Provided Housing vs. Private Rentals

1. Company Housing (Hostels / Shared Apartments)

Many recruitment contracts stipulate that the employer provides accommodation:

  • Hygiene & Standards: Under Romanian occupational standards, employer housing must provide adequate heating, running hot water, electricity, clean bedding, and sanitary kitchen facilities.
  • Deduction Limits: If your contract specifies free housing, the company cannot suddenly deduct rent from your paycheck. Any housing deduction must be explicitly documented in your initial contract or an annex.
  • Proof of Space: Your employer must provide an official document (Contract de comodat or Declarație de luare în spațiu) confirming to IGI that you reside at this address.

2. Renting a Private Apartment or Shared Room

If you decide to rent your own accommodation with fellow workers:

  • Popular Rental Websites in Romania: Storia.ro, Imobiliare.ro, and local Facebook expat/rental groups.

What to Prepare When Renting Privately

Understanding Utility Bills in Romania

Rent in Romania usually does not include utilities. You must budget separately for:

  1. Building Maintenance (Întreținere): Monthly charge calculated by the building tenants' association (Asociația de Proprietari). Includes common area heating, hallway cleaning, trash collection, elevator electricity, and water consumption.
  2. Electricity (Curent Electric / Enel, Electrica, Hidroelectrica): Billed monthly based on meter readings.
  3. Natural Gas (Gaz Natural / Engie, E.ON): In winter, heating costs rise significantly. If your apartment has an individual heating unit (Centrală termică de apartament), check monthly consumption.
  4. Internet / Wi-Fi (Digi, Orange): Typically fast and very affordable (approx. ⚠️ [VERIFY: Average monthly fiber optic home internet cost, e.g. 30 to 45 RON per month]).

Common Pitfalls & Mistakes

1. Renting from a landlord who refuses to register with ANAF or help with IGI

  • The Danger: If your landlord refuses to provide a property deed copy (Extras de Carte Funciară) or notarized agreement (Declarație de luare în spațiu), IGI will reject your residence permit renewal!
  • Rule: Before paying any deposit, ask directly: "Are you willing to provide the necessary documents for my IGI residence permit address registration?"

2. Paying cash without signed receipts

  • The Danger: Unscrupulous landlords claiming you never paid the rent.
  • Rule: Always pay rent and utilities by bank transfer into the landlord's account with the description: "Chirie luna [Month]" (Rent for month X).

3. Subletting without the owner's knowledge

  • Moving extra roommates into an apartment without the owner's written permission violates the lease and risks immediate eviction and security deposit forfeiture.
