Work Permit & Residence Permit Renewals in Romania (Permis de Ședere)

Overview

As a non-EU citizen from Nepal, your legal right to stay in Romania depends on your Single Permit (Permis de Ședere în scop de muncă).

Your initial visa allows you into the country, but the residence permit card is the actual document that grants legal stay (usually for 1 year, renewable annually). If your permit expires without an in-progress renewal, you risk deportation, fines, and an EU entry ban.

  2. Mutual Consent vs. Without Consent: If you leave before this period without written company approval, IGI may reject the new employer's work permit application.
  3. Grace Period for Unemployed Workers: If your contract is terminated, you have ⚠️ [VERIFY: Grace period duration in days to find a new employer and notify IGI before residence right is revoked, usually 90 days].

WARNING ON "ESCAPE" AGENTS: Never pay illegal brokers who promise to "transfer" you to another European country (such as Germany or Portugal) on your Romanian permit. A Romanian work permit only authorizes work within Romania. Leaving Romania unlawfully leads to arrest at borders and an expulsion ban across the entire Schengen Zone.

Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

1. Assuming the employer handles everything automatically

  • Mistake: Workers wait passively, only to discover their visa expired because HR forgot the deadline.
  • Solution: Check your passport visa expiration date on Day 1. Set a calendar reminder on your phone for 45 days before expiry. Ask HR in writing: "Has my IGI appointment been filed?"

2. Not keeping the IGI submission registration slip

  • Mistake: When IGI accepts your renewal file, they issue a registration certificate (Adeverință de depunere). If you do not have this slip, police cannot verify that your legal status is pending.
  • Solution: Take a photo of the IGI filing slip immediately. Carry this paper with your passport at all times while awaiting your card.

3. Change of address without informing IGI

  • Mistake: Moving to a new apartment or hostel without reporting the address change to IGI within ⚠️ [VERIFY: Legal deadline to report address change to IGI, typically 15 or 30 days].
  • Solution: Always submit a notice of address change to your territorial IGI inspectorate to avoid administrative fines.
