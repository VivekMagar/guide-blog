Overview

Learning basic Romanian phrases shows respect to your colleagues, prevents workplace misunderstandings, and helps you communicate directly with supervisors, doctors, and immigration officials.

Romanian is a Latin-based Romance language. Most words are pronounced phonetically as they are spelled.
