Banking & Prepaid SIM Cards in Romania: A Guide for Expats

Overview

Opening a bank account in Romania and securing a mobile SIM card with abundant data are two of the first tasks you must complete upon arrival. You need a Romanian bank account (IBAN) to receive your salary legally, and a mobile phone number for 2-factor authentication, mobile banking apps, and contacting family in Nepal.

Part 1: Mobile SIM Cards in Romania

Romania has among the fastest and cheapest mobile internet in the European Union. Prepaid SIM cards (Cartelă PrePay) are recommended for newcomers because they require no credit checks or long-term contract lock-ins.

Major Telecom Operators

  • Digi (RCS & RDS): Known for the lowest prices and strong city coverage.
  • Orange Romania: Highest nationwide 4G/5G coverage, very reliable in rural work areas.
  • Vodafone Romania: Popular international network, solid prepaid packages.
  • Telekom Romania Mobile: Good budget options.

Part 2: Opening a Romanian Bank Account

Romanian employers generally pay wages by bank transfer directly into an IBAN (International Bank Account Number) registered in your name.

Major Romanian Banks

  • Banca Transilvania (BT): The largest bank in Romania, widely used by employers for payroll.
  • BCR (Banca Comercială Română / Erste Group): Modern digital banking app (George).
  • BRD (Groupe Société Générale): Wide branch network across provincial towns.
  • Raiffeisen Bank & ING Bank: Popular for digital services, though some branches have stricter onboarding policies for non-EU citizens.

How to Avoid Unnecessary Banking Fees

  1. Ask for a Basic Account (Cont de Plăți cu Servicii de Bază): Under EU Directive 2014/92/EU, all consumers legally resident in an EU member state are entitled to a basic bank account with free or very low maintenance fees.
  2. ATM Withdrawals: Withdraw cash only from ATMs (Bancomat) operated by your own bank. Withdrawing from competitor bank ATMs may incur fees between ⚠️ [VERIFY: Standard interbank ATM withdrawal commission fee in RON, e.g. 2.5 to 5 RON + percentage].
  3. Card Currency: Always choose a debit card denominated in RON (Romanian Leu). If you need to make purchases or transfers in EUR, you can open a secondary EUR sub-account inside your banking app.
