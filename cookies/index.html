Cookie Policy

DRAFT NOTICE: This document is a template prepared for Sathi Romania. It must be reviewed by a legal specialist prior to production launch.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you access websites. They are commonly used to remember preferences, enhance navigation, and analyze aggregate visitor traffic.

2. Our Approach to Cookies & Tracking

Sathi Romania is designed to be lightweight, privacy-first, and high-performance, especially for users on slower mobile internet connections:

  • No Invasive Third-Party Tracking: We do not embed Facebook Pixels, Google DoubleClick tracking, or invasive behavioral advertising networks.
  • Privacy-Centric Analytics: Our analytics configuration uses cookieless measurement (such as self-hosted Plausible or Umami). These tools measure aggregate page views without creating individual visitor profiles or setting tracking cookies.

3. What We Store Locally

We use minimal browser local storage (localStorage) solely for functional user preferences:

Storage NamePurposeDurationEssential?
sathi_langRemembers your chosen language preference (en or ne) across sessionsPersistent (until cleared)Functional
sathi_themeRemembers your manual light/dark mode preferencePersistent (until cleared)Functional

Under the ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC) and Romanian Law No. 506/2004, strictly necessary and functional cookies that exist solely to provide a service explicitly requested by the user do not require prior opt-in consent banners.

4. Third-Party Links

When you click external links (such as official Romanian government portals like IGI or commercial money transfer providers), those external websites may deploy their own cookies under their independent privacy policies.

5. How to Manage or Disable Storage in Your Browser

You can configure your browser to reject cookies or clear local storage at any time:

  • Chrome / Android: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Safari / iOS: Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
