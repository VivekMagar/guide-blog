🛡️ 4-Step DOFE & Agency Verification Checklist for Nepali Workers

Never hand over money to an unauthorized individual agent or sub-agent without verifying these 4 steps first:

1
Verify the LT Number: Check the government Lot (LT) Number on the official Department of Foreign Employment portal (dofe.gov.np).
2
Confirm Agency License: Make sure the manpower company is currently active, licensed by DOFE, and not blacklisted.
3
Never Pay Advance Cash: Genuine Romanian recruitment begins with an employer-sponsored Work Permit (Aviz de Angajare). Advance cash demands are 90%+ fraudulent.
4
Bank Transfer & Official Receipt: Always pay through official bank channels with an authorized company invoice. Keep all WhatsApp chats, payment slips, and contracts.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, verified answers to the most common questions about working legally, salaries, overtime, taxes, and living in Romania.

What is the typical net take-home salary for Nepali workers in Romania? ▼

For general or unskilled positions (factory packaging, janitorial, kitchen assistant), the starting take-home net salary is typically 2,600 to 3,500 RON/month (approx. रु. ८०,००० – १,०८,००० NPR). For skilled trades (construction masons, carpenters, electricians, forklift operators), net wages range from 4,000 to 5,200+ RON/month (approx. रु. १,२५,००० – १,६०,०००+ NPR).

Regular overtime and night shifts add additional income. If your employer provides free accommodation and monthly meal vouchers, your monthly savings will be significantly higher.

What is the legal overtime pay rate under Romanian labor law? ▼

Under the Romanian Labour Code (Codul Muncii), standard full-time employment is 40 hours per week (8 hours/day). Overtime hours must legally be compensated with extra pay:

  • Weekday Overtime: Minimum +75% on top of your hourly wage (e.g. if base is 25 RON/hr, overtime pays 43.75 RON/hr), or compensated with paid time off within 90 days.
  • Weekend & Public Holiday Overtime: Minimum +100% (double base pay — e.g. 25 RON/hr base pays 50 RON/hr).

Overtime is legally capped at an average of 8 hours per week (maximum 48 total hours in any single working week).

How much tax and social security is deducted from my Romanian salary? ▼

Mandatory employee payroll deductions in Romania total approximately 35% to 42% of gross salary:

  • CAS (Pension / Social Insurance): 25% of gross contribution base.
  • CASS (Public Health Insurance): 10% of gross contribution base.
  • PIT (Personal Income Tax): 10% on the remaining taxable income (after deducting CAS, CASS, and personal deductions).

Full-time employees on the national minimum wage benefit from a 300 RON tax-free allowance from their tax base, plus additional deductions if you declare dependent family members.

What is the typical RON to NPR exchange rate and best way to send money home? ▼

The mid-market exchange rate is currently around 1 RON ≈ 31.0 – 31.2 NPR (and 1 EUR ≈ 5.0 RON). Digital money transfer platforms such as Wise and Remitly, or direct mobile banking remittances, typically provide mid-market rates and lower fees compared to cash exchange kiosks.

Check our Live Transfer Comparison Tool before sending money to see real recipient payout amounts in Nepal across all providers.

How much recruitment fee is legal to pay an agency to go to Romania? ▼

Under Nepal Department of Foreign Employment (DOFE) rules, agencies cannot charge arbitrary fees. Legitimate administrative costs (medical examination, insurance, consular visa fees, DOFE welfare fund, pre-departure orientation, and air ticket) typically total NPR 2,00,000 to 3,50,000.

Warning: Never pay advance cash to an individual broker without an officially verified Lot (LT) Number from DOFE. Anyone demanding 5 to 8+ Lakhs or cash without a formal bank deposit slip is operating fraudulently. Report fraud to the DOFE Helpline at 1145 or Central Investigation Bureau at 01-4411550.

How long does the entire Romania work visa process take from Nepal? ▼

The legal employment process typically requires 4 to 6 months from start to arrival:

  1. Work Permit (Aviz de Muncă): Romanian employer obtains authorization from the General Inspectorate for Immigration (IGI) (30–60 days).
  2. Long-Stay Visa (D/AM Visa): Agency applies online via evisa.mae.ro (30–45 days).
  3. Consular Appointment: Biometrics and passport submission at the Romanian Embassy in New Delhi or consular missions (30+ days).
  4. Final Labor Permit & Departure: DOFE labor approval (श्रम स्वीकृति) in Kathmandu followed by flight deployment.
Which jobs have the highest demand and quota in Romania? ▼

Romania currently issues the highest contingents of foreign work authorizations for:

  • Warehouses & Logistics: Order pickers, packagers, and forklift drivers for e-commerce and retail chains (eMAG, Mega Image, Lidl).
  • Manufacturing & Factories: Automotive components, electronics assembly, food processing, and garment production.
  • Construction: Masons, carpenters, scaffolders, electricians, and general laborers.
  • Delivery & Couriers: Food courier services (Glovo, Tazz, Bolt) and commercial van drivers.
  • Hospitality: Kitchen helpers, dishwashers, janitorial, and hotel housekeeping.
