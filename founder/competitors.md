# Competitor Analysis: Nepalese Workers in Romania

## Scope

The market is the information and community journey of a Nepalese worker considering,
preparing for, or living in Romania. This is not a local country guide only: buyers
may solve the problem through job boards, government sources, social groups,
manpower agencies, remittance apps, or scattered articles.

## Close-competitor conclusion

Yes, this market is not empty. The closest verifiable guide-like competitor found is
the **Romania Salary & Tax Calculator for Nepali Workers**. It is Nepali-language,
free, and specifically combines wage, tax, overtime, living-cost, scam-prevention,
job, visa and remittance themes. It appears to be a one-page tool rather than a
broad persistent bilingual guide.

A second repository, **NEPAL TO ROMANIA VISA PROCESS**, is narrow and currently
appears to be a repository-level visa guide rather than a full user-facing
information platform.

No well-established public product that matches the full combination of English +
Nepali, newcomer checklist, immigration guidance, community, salary tools and live
RON-to-NPR comparisons was found among the public searches and GitHub repository
searches completed here. This is a limitation, not proof that no such site exists.

## Price range

For information and tools, the direct and indirect public sources are generally free.
Private recruitment, remittance and remittance advice can have fees that vary by
provider and transaction. No public comparable subscription price was found.

## Positioning map

**Axis 1: Broad guide -> Single-purpose tool**  
**Axis 2: Community/peer-led -> Official/authoritative**

- Sathi Romania: broad guide, bilingual, partially community-led, with official-source
  links intended to provide authority.
- Salary/tax calculator: narrow single-purpose tool, community-oriented and
  Nepali-language.
- DOFE and IGI: authoritative official sources, narrow channel, limited user
  experience and no matching bilingual community layer.
- eJobs, BestJobs and EURES: job discovery only; weak or absent migration,
  newcomer and remittance context.
- Facebook group: strong peer context but no structured navigation or authority.
- Recruitment agencies: high transaction value but often opaque and potentially
  associated with the scam risk that workers are trying to avoid.
- Remitly and Wise: strong remittance functionality but no newcomer or immigration
  coverage.

## Public-review assessment

No reliable recurring review themes could be verified for Sathi Romania's close
competitors. The calculator is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the job boards and
government sites do not expose a comparable set of public reviews through the
accessible pages. Therefore, no complaint claims or review quotes are included.

## Gap

The strongest gap is to be the **trusted, structured, bilingual operating guide**
around the entire journey, not another isolated calculator or job board.

Evidence supports owning:

1. **A single triaged newcomer flow**: first 30 days, permit, housing, banking,
   SIM, work rights, insurance and doctor access.
2. **One-place verification**: make official links and source dates visible beside
   every claim instead of mixing estimates and law.
3. **Bilingual community + moderation**: real worker experiences linked to the
   relevant guide section, with scams and unverified stories clearly labeled.
4. **Tools that remain connected**: wages, living costs, savings and remittance
   choices can feed a single decision instead of separate calculators.
5. **Clear risk boundaries**: identify which steps are official requirements,
   which are estimates, and when a lawyer or government authority should confirm.

The broad guide has a meaningful differentiation opportunity if it keeps the
calculator's Nepalese-language relevance while outperforming official sites on
navigation, date freshness and plain-language explanation.

## Threat

The calculator site is the fastest likely competitor to copy the wage/tax/remittance
area because it is simple, focused and GitHub-hosted. Facebook groups can copy
community and lived-experience volume faster than a website, while recruitment
agencies can bundle advice into placement services. The biggest defensive move is
frequent source verification, a visible update ledger, and structured bilingual
content that is much harder to replace than one calculator.
